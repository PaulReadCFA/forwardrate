Implied Forward Rate Equation

A rearrangement of Equation 26 expresses the implied forward rate as a function of a longer-term and a short-term spot rate (in this case we focus on a two-year spot rate and a one-year spot rate). The implied forward rate (covering the period one year from now to two years from now) is therefore computed as follows:

$$\color{#7a46ff}{F_{1,2}} = \frac{(1+\color{#dc2626}{r_2})^2}{(1+\color{#047857}{r_1})} - 1$$
